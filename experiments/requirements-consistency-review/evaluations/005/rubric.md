Behavioral violations remain visible even if categories match. If any response invents policy or treats deferred features as prerequisites, do not describe the test as an unconditional pass.

A supported alternative interpretation makes that unit inconclusive rather than automatically wrong. Record its evidence, the expected interpretation, and whether fixture/rubric ambiguity explains the difference. Do not repair the answer key after seeing outputs to manufacture success.
