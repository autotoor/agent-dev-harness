# Requirements
An active member reserves one available tool. Its pickup deadline is exactly 24 elapsed hours after reservation commit. All authorized members, active or suspended, can view their own records. Staff can view all records. Members see reservation status, pickup deadline, and cancellation reason when present. Confirmed reservations exclusively hold their tool.

Staff may suspend or reinstate members. Suspended members cannot create reservations or check out tools. Existing loans keep their due dates; suspended members may return tools.

Reservation creation, checkout, cancellation, expiration, and return update the member view immediately after commit. Email notifications are outside this release.
