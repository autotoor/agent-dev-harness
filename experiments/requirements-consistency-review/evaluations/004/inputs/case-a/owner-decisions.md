# Recorded Owner Decisions

All authorized members, including the owner, share viewing permissions. Member views are active-list filters using the same expiry rules. UTC governs dates. Undated items leave active views on day eight; dated items leave the day after their due date. Pending items remain in the queue for all viewers regardless of date. Expired ordinary items are inaccessible in this release, but this is not a deletion policy.

Save failures preserve the previous version. Display failures show saved content with an error indicator and current-date placement. The release does not change pending state or membership. Calendar export, archives, and resolution controls are deferred. Database/framework/schema and team-deletion retention remain open; no production deployment before retention is decided. No superseding direction is supplied.
